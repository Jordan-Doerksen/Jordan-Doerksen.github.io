Plate 01✦About

A field guide to the person

My first life was in Oshawa, Ontario. I left it to go find the next one — roofing crews, arborist work, summers on a homestead in the mountains of B.C., building websites for people, and odd experiments inside videogame modding tools. The constant through all of it: a need to make things.

Then the railway. Almost two years inside the training system — through layoffs, lockouts, and restarts — and more than a hundred runs driving freight through the mountains. The people I met out there changed my life forever. When that chapter closed, I started over again, this time in Winnipeg.

Warcraft III and StarCraft taught me systems thinking before any textbook did; The Lord of the Rings and Princess Mononoke taught me what systems are for. This site is an observatory of the things I make and the places they come from. Click around — some doors are hidden.

-- Spawn a coin somewhere in the arena; speed up each wave
function SpawnCoin()
  local x = GetRandomReal(GetRectMinX(gg_rct_Arena),
                          GetRectMaxX(gg_rct_Arena))
  local y = GetRandomReal(GetRectMinY(gg_rct_Arena),
                          GetRectMaxY(gg_rct_Arena))
  CreateItem(FourCC('gold'), x, y)
  local delay = math.max(0.25, 0.85 - 0.01 * wave)
  TimerStart(CreateTimer(), delay, false, SpawnCoin)
end
