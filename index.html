About

A field guide to the person

I grew up in Winnipeg and never quite picked one world to live in. By day I've conducted freight through the Rockies for CN Rail; by night I write code, build 3D-printed things, and chase guitar tones that sound like weather.

Warcraft III and StarCraft taught me systems thinking before any textbook did. The Lord of the Rings and Princess Mononoke taught me what those systems are for. I tend to see the good in people first — and in animals before that.

This site is an archive of the things I make and the places they come from. Click around. Some doors are hidden.

-- Spawn a coin somewhere in the arena; speed up each wave
function SpawnCoin()
  local x = GetRandomReal(GetRectMinX(gg_rct_Arena),
                          GetRectMaxX(gg_rct_Arena))
  local y = GetRandomReal(GetRectMinY(gg_rct_Arena),
                          GetRectMaxY(gg_rct_Arena))
  CreateItem(FourCC('gold'), x, y)
  local delay = math.max(0.25, 0.85 - 0.01 * wave)
  TimerStart(CreateTimer(), delay, false, SpawnCoin)
end
