how it started

“Wouldn't it be funny if we…”

Meme-laden on the outside, dead serious about the food on the inside.

It started in 2020 with a dumb idea between cousins. “Wouldn't it be funny if we…” — and Nyk Bielak and Stefan Lytwyn were off. First came Kosmo's Space Cowboy Food Cantina, a pandemic ghost kitchen slinging burgers, fried chicken and mac out of the German Society of Winnipeg's North End basement.

Cooking in that basement, they pulled in Jesse Angers — who'd worked alongside Lytwyn at Deer + Almond — and the crew (Mischa Decter included) found their bordering-on-absurd voice. By summer 2022 they wanted to see the faces behind the online orders, so they took the keys to a little shop at 513 Osborne St and pivoted hard toward Italian submarine sandwiches.

For the longest time there wasn't even a sign — just a painting of a donkey dragging a sandwich, and a great view of the cemetery across the street. The menu reads like a stoner wrote it. The subs taste like people who cooked in fine kitchens made them. Both things are true.

“Winnipeg will always be a blue-collar town,” says Lytwyn, “and that's how we see ourselves — a blue-collar restaurant that's a little off the beaten path.” The whole thing, as he puts it, was born out of one big risk.
