Our Story

A Winnipeg classic that never went out of style.

Tucked into the bustle of Pembina Highway, Garwood Grill has been quietly feeding Winnipeg the way grandma intended — generous plates, fair prices, and a friendly face behind the counter who remembers your usual.

There's no fuss here. Just the smell of bacon on the flat-top, a pot of coffee that never runs dry, and the kind of pizza, steak and burgers that have kept families coming back for decades.

Come as a stranger, leave as a regular. That's the Garwood way.
