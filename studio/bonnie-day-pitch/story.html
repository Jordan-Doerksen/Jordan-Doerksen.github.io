Our Story · A Play in Two Acts

It began with
a good idea

[ a family, a long debate over dinner, and one happy verdict ]

Bonnie Day was something our David used to say. After the back-and-forth over where to eat, he'd land the decision with two words — bonnie day — and that was that. A small double entendre: in our ancestors' Scots, a beautiful one; said aloud, the French bonne idée, a good idea. Both were always true.

[ Westminster Avenue. Evening. A door opens. ]
Act I — The Room

We opened in 2021 on Westminster Avenue, in the room Wolseley knew as Ruby West — and before that, a beloved neighbourhood bookstore and café. We kept what we loved: the candlelight, the closeness, the feeling that you'd wandered into a friend's dining room mid-conversation.

By day the space is a café — Never Better Coffee on the bar, scones cooling, the morning unhurried. By night the candles come up and it turns resto: a short, generous menu, a thoughtful cocktail list, and a chair for everyone at the table.

Act II — The Table

Chef Pamela Kirkpatrick cooks comfort food with intention — nourishing, and deliberately inclusive. Vegan, gluten-free, omnivore, sweet tooth: the menu was built so no one at the table ever has to ask twice. The point was never to impress you. It was to feed you like family.

"Come for the candlelight; stay because it feels like somewhere you already knew."

In Memory · The Namesake

David King

Playwright · 1949 – 2021

An uncle and a father to us, and the reason this place has a name. Across his life David wrote twenty-five premiere productions and won two Jessie Awards — for Garage Sale and Life Skills — alongside years writing for CBC variety in Vancouver and Winnipeg, and film, including the Gemini-winning For Gentleman Only. He loved a good story and a fuller table. Bonnie Day is our way of keeping both going.

"He'd have called it a good idea."
