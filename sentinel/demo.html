Macro Bias strip

One-glance verdict (RISK-ON / RISK-OFF / MIXED / NEUTRAL) built from three independent streams: news sentiment, options skew, and order-book flow. Each chip shows its own lean and the data behind it.
Why it matters: when independent streams agree, the environment has a clear character; when they conflict, that disagreement is itself information — expect chop.

ES Futures

Live front-month ES price with point and percent change versus the session reference close.
Why it matters: the anchor for everything else on screen — every other panel is context around this number.

SPX Volatility

ATM implied volatility plus 25-delta put and call IV from SPX options, and the put–call skew between them. Skew above zero means downside protection costs more than upside exposure.
Why it matters: IV sets how far moves can run; rising put skew shows institutions paying up for crash protection — fear priced in before it shows in price.

Structure · VWAP & Prior Day

Session VWAP (the volume-weighted average price — the day's "fair price" so far), prior-day high and low, and whether price is trading above, below, or at VWAP.
Why it matters: these are the reference levels the most participants watch. Above VWAP, buyers are in control on the session; PDH/PDL are where stops cluster and breakouts get tested.

ES Depth of Market

The live order book: resting bid and ask size at each price level, with the OBI gauge summarizing which side is heavier (−1 ask-stacked … +1 bid-stacked).
Why it matters: shows where liquidity is waiting before price gets there. A stacked side can act as a wall — or as fuel if it pulls. Needs the CME depth subscription; falls back honestly to level 1.

Tape · Volume Delta

Every trade is classified by who crossed the spread: lifting the ask = aggressive buying, hitting the bid = aggressive selling. CVD is the session's running net of that aggression.
Why it matters: price tells you where the market is; CVD tells you who is pushing. Rising price on falling CVD is a divergence worth respecting.

Absorption

Watches for a narrow price band soaking heavy one-sided aggression without giving way — the footprint of a large passive order (often an iceberg). BID ABSORBING = sellers keep hitting a bid that won't drop; ASK ABSORBING = buyers keep lifting an ask that won't break.
Why it matters: someone big is taking the other side quietly. Bid absorption is bullish context, ask absorption bearish — and the level itself becomes a line in the sand: if it finally breaks, the absorbed side often capitulates.

Setup Conditions

A read-only checklist that restates every stream as a pass / warn / fail condition: vol environment, skew extreme, news bias, event risk, VWAP structure, tape lean, book lean, absorption.
Why it matters: a fast pre-trade scan of whether the environment supports the idea. It is deliberately context only — never a signal; the decision is always yours.

Macro Signal Feed

World headlines filtered for macro relevance and classified risk-on / risk-off by a local AI, each with a confidence grade and one-line reasoning. The heartbeat distinguishes "quiet world" from "broken pipe".
Why it matters: macro shocks hit ES before most people read the story. The tally shows whether the recent flow of news leans one way — and the reasoning lets you judge the call yourself.
