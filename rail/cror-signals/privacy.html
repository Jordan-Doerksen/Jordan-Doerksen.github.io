Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026.

CROR Signals is built privacy-first. It collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your study progress — which aspects you’ve seen, your Leitner boxes, your streak, and your settings — is saved locally on your device only (in the app’s local storage). It never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the app’s data, or using “Reset progress” inside the app, erases it.

Network access

The app works fully offline. The only optional outbound link is the “Buy me a coffee” tip link, which opens an external page in your browser if you choose to tap it.

Children

The app is a study aid suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.
