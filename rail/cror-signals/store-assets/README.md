## Listing copy

**App name:** CROR Signals — Rail Signal Trainer

**Short description (≤80 chars):**
> Every Canadian rail signal, drawn. Learn, quiz & master CROR aspects. Free.

**Full description:**
> Learn to read Canadian Rail Operating Rules (CROR) block & interlocking signals — the real way,
> by sight. Every aspect from Rules 405–440 is **drawn**, lamp for lamp, not buried in text.
>
> • **Learn** — browse every indication, flip for the meaning and a plain-English note.
> • **Quiz** — see a signal, name the indication; wrong answers explain themselves.
> • **Build** — assemble the lamp stack from the name (reverse practice).
> • **Family drill** — focus one signal family at a time.
> • **Timed exam** — exam conditions, scored, with a full review.
> • **Progress** — spaced repetition, a daily streak, and your weak spots surfaced.
>
> Built for new conductors and engineers, and anyone brushing up. Every aspect is checked against
> the **January 2025 CROR** — names and meanings are the rulebook text. (No invented aspects, and
> no “A” plate — that’s US practice; Canada uses DV / R / L.)
>
> **Free. No ads. No accounts. No tracking. Works fully offline.**
>
> A study aid — not the official rulebook. Always verify against the current CROR and your
> company instructions.
