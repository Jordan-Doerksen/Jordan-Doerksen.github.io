SYNTHETIC · a synthetic feed and staged outages; nothing on this card is generated

Stands for the options skew engine. It reads the chain once per refresh interval and publishes one word with the number. When the chain goes away it holds the last good read up to the stale limit, marks it suspect and prints no number; past the limit it prints n/a and says why. A second with no read is a shaded gap in the line, never a zero.
