    The 0DTE sky

    SYNTHETIC · a synthetic feed and staged outages; nothing on this card is generated

    Stands for the options skew engine. It reads the chain once per refresh interval and publishes one word with the number. When the chain goes away it holds the last good read up to the stale limit, marks it suspect and prints no number; past the limit it prints n/a and says why. A second with no read is a shaded gap in the line, never a zero.

    The operator's ring

    SYNTHETIC · the cycles are staged; the words are the desk's own; no figure on this card is generated

    Small programs mind the desk and each says what it did. The doctor grades it. The watchdog restarts it, or stands down when the stop was deliberate. The tape backup moves recordings off the machine and refuses when a tape would shrink. The launcher's update pulls new code and proves it before keeping it. One pass of each is staged here, in the desk's own words.
